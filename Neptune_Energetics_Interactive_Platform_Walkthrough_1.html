From wasted heat to dispatched power.

Neptune Energetics installs waste heat power plants on site at data centers, thermal power plants, manufacturing plants, and refineries. An agentic control system reads the grid and the facility in real time, then decides — for every kilowatt — whether to offset consumption or sell to the grid.

Cross-cutting controls

✓
Static hardware core
Evaporation, mixing, and pressurization happen in fixed geometry — the turbine is the only moving part. This design enhances modularity, which reduces manufacturing and maintenance costs.
✓
Safety interlocks
Pressure and temperature limits are enforced in hardware, independent of the dispatch agent.
✓
Telemetry audit trail
Every dispatch decision and payment is logged and traceable back to a grid-price snapshot.
